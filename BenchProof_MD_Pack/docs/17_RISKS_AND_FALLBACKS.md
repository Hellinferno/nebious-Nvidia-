# Risks, cut lines, and fallback decisions

Research-driven breadth is a major schedule risk. The fallback is a complete small assurance product with explicit limitations, not a claim that all control-plane ideas have been implemented.

## Risk register

| Risk | Trigger to observe | Action/owner | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Cut order

Cut P2 agent adapters, model routing, extra languages and graph animations first. Then cut optional head-to-head agent trials and extra external cases. Reduce P1 suite scope explicitly only before measurement: choose R6/V6; leave unavailable G8/F6/W8/M12 NOT_RUN and describe implemented checks separately.

If needed, retain simple source-hash freshness and hard repeated-failure stopping while dropping richer decision ledger/recovery. Mutations may be NOT_RUN in P0; never label their strength adequate. A restricted but complete protected gate is the core.

Never remove meaningful required NVIDIA runtime use, candidate isolation, human-owned constraints, independent acceptance, clean/rejected states, ownership, required-check unknown handling, basic export, license/access or submission obligations while still claiming the full product.

## Time-slip protocol

At a missed daily gate, update progress with actual blocker and hours. Resume the earliest unmet P0 dependency, cut later breadth and preserve release days. At 10/17/24 October reviews, select scope based on executed evidence and usable budget, not the number of planned features.

If required execution/isolation never works, mark the release blocked for live execution and build truthful review-only material while resolving the provider path. Do not claim that an evidence viewer alone fulfills the planned working-app story.

## Seven-session P0 recovery

| Session | Output and gate |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Actual inference/runner and approved AC/MC/clean development fixtures |
| 2 | Typed constraints, immutable source hashes, minimal AST/manifest impact |
| 3 | Bounded agent repair and protected fresh acceptance; invalid candidate rejected |
| 4 | Usable UI, limits/ownership, clean/blocked paths, export and fresh replay |
| 5 | Explicit R6/V6 measurement if feasible, failures/usage and deployment |
| 6 | Clean-clone/license/access, three rehearsals, genuine short video |
| 7 | Accurate entry, feedback, reviewed links and actual submitted status |

This is a cut-scope recovery route, not a promise that seven sessions are sufficient regardless of blockers. Preserve safety/integrity and report actual suite/feature coverage.

## Decision record

Record observed trigger, options actually tested, selected route, acceptance evidence, cost/scope/security/schedule effect, documents changed and revisit date. A hypothetical fallback remains proposed until its preflight passes. Retain the submitted-release identities through any recovery.
