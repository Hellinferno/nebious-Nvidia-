## Official summary

The selected track is **Coding and Agentic Engineering**. Entry requires a working application with a runtime Token Factory inference call or Nebius AI Cloud execution and at least one NVIDIA open-source model. Track fit must be substantive.

Submit a working demo/test-build URL, description, track choice, public open-source repository with a detectable license and setup README, public YouTube demonstration, and tool feedback. Make the video shorter than three minutes. Explain substantial in-period changes if reusing a pre-existing project. Respect third-party rights. Provide free reviewer access until judging ends.

Eligibility includes local age of majority and geographic/conflict exclusions; Ravi must check the complete terms. The four equally weighted judging areas are implementation, design, impact, and idea quality.
